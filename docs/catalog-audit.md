# Catalog Audit

## Catalog policy

The product catalog in this project is static. Prices are not scraped, stored, or requested from live APIs.

## Review cadence

Manually review the catalog at least once per quarter.

## Audit checklist

- Confirm each record still represents the intended capacity tier.
- Confirm pump and gravity-drain flags are still accurate.
- Confirm low-temperature suitability claims still match current manufacturer marketing.
- Confirm affiliate URLs still route to the intended retailer searches or replacement links.
- Confirm image placeholders are still appropriate if product-specific creative is not available.
- Confirm disclosure language and link strategy still match site policy.

## Why this matters

Static catalogs age over time. Quarterly review prevents outdated product assumptions from driving recommendations or affiliate placements.
