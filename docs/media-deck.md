# Media Deck

## Positioning

Old vs New Dehumidifier Rating Calculator helps homeowners interpret old pint labels, current DOE ratings, and basement-specific drainage needs without relying on scraped ecommerce data.

## Audience

- Homeowners replacing an older basement dehumidifier
- Shoppers comparing 20-22, 30-35, and 45-50 pint DOE models
- Affiliate publishers building educational buying guides

## Key messages

- Older pint labels do not map one-to-one with current DOE labels.
- Basement temperature and drainage setup materially affect the right product shortlist.
- Very large or flooded spaces should trigger professional assessment instead of a casual portable-unit recommendation.

## Content angles

- Explain why old 70-pint units often compare to current 50-pint basement models.
- Compare pump versus gravity-drain setups for basements without floor drains.
- Show why cooler basements need careful low-temperature product matching.

## Monetization notes

- Product cards are driven by a static catalog and affiliate-ready outbound URLs.
- Disclosure copy appears near product links and again in the footer.
- Analytics support can be switched between console, Plausible, GA4, or none.
