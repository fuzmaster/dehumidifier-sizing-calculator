Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Calc My Dehumidifier is a free, static web calculator that helps homeowners pick a modern dehumidifier to replace older basement units. This page explains exactly what is and isn't collected when you use it.

What this site does not collect

  • No account, no login, no email, no password.
  • No name, address, or phone number.
  • No free-text input — every form field is either a number (square footage) or a dropdown selection.
  • The calculator runs entirely in your browser. Your inputs are never sent to a server we own.

What is collected

The site uses two privacy-respecting analytics services to understand which features get used:

  • Google Analytics 4 — collects standard web analytics (page views, anonymized IP-derived location, device/browser type) and the non-identifying events listed below. Sets _ga / _ga_* cookies. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Vercel Web Analytics — privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics for page views and performance metrics. See Vercel's Privacy Policy.

The events we send include:

  • calculator_started, calculator_completed — that the calculator was used.
  • The numeric square footage you entered and the dropdown selections (humidity, temperature, drainage, budget, confusion mode).
  • The recommended capacity tier and confidence level the calculator produced.
  • affiliate_card_clicked / affiliate_cta_clicked — which product card you clicked, if any.

Affiliate links

Product recommendation cards include affiliate links to retailers such as Amazon and Home Depot. As an Amazon Associate, the site earns from qualifying purchases. Retailer pricing is not live and may change between visits. Affiliate links are marked with rel="sponsored".

Cookies

Google Analytics sets first-party cookies (typically named _ga and _ga_*) to distinguish unique sessions. Vercel Analytics does not set cookies. You can block these via your browser's cookie controls or any standard tracker-blocking extension without affecting the calculator's functionality.

Third parties

Other than the analytics services above and the retailer links you click, no third party receives data from this site.

Data retention and your rights

Analytics data is retained per the providers' default policies. Because no account or identifying information is collected, there is nothing tied to you personally that we can export or delete on request. If you would like analytics data associated with your visit erased, the most direct path is to use Google's own opt-out mechanisms or clear your browser cookies.

Children

This site is a home-improvement shopping guide intended for adults. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date at the top will change. Material changes will also be noted in the site footer.
